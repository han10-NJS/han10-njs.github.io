Frequently Asked Questions

What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal, which also includes all of the accumulated interest from previous periods. This creates a snowball effect that accelerates wealth accumulation over time.

What is the 4% Rule in Financial Independence?

The 4% rule originated from the Trinity Study. It suggests that if you withdraw 4% of your total portfolio value in your first retirement year and adjust for inflation each subsequent year, your nest egg has a high probability of lasting at least 30 years.

The Power of the Rule of 72

The Rule of 72 is a quick, useful formula to estimate the number of years required to double your money at a given annual rate of return:

Years to Double = 72 / Rate

Example: An 8% return doubles your investment in approximately 9 years (72 / 8 = 9).
